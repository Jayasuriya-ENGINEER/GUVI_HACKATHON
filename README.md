# Healthy Bites

Healthy Bites is a nutrition-focused food delivery platform designed for fitness enthusiasts, gym-goers, athletes, and anyone committed to a healthier lifestyle. It offers freshly prepared, macro-balanced meals in both vegetarian and non-vegetarian options, supporting muscle growth, fat loss, and everyday wellness.

---

## Features

- Goal-based healthy meal suggestions (muscle gain, fat-loss, maintenance)
- High-protein meal packs (Veg & Non-Veg)
- Clean and user-friendly UI design
- Healthy drinks & snacks add-ons
- Order and delivery workflow (design stage)
- Portion-controlled meal packaging

---

## Purpose

To make high-protein, nutrient-balanced eating simple and accessible for fitness-focused individuals.
